# Project 04 – Decathlon Retail Performance Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Decathlon Retail Performance Analytics  
**Industry:** Retail – Sports & Fitness  
**Domain:** Sales Performance, Retail Analytics and Business Intelligence  
**Primary Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Project Type:** End-to-End Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Project

This project focuses on analyzing Decathlon's retail sales performance, profitability, product contribution and regional business performance using interactive Power BI dashboards.

The objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights that support management decision-making and improve visibility into sales and profitability.

## 2. Business Problem Statement

Retail businesses operate across multiple regions, stores, product categories and sales channels. Monitoring overall business performance becomes challenging when sales and operational data are distributed across multiple datasets.

Business stakeholders need a centralized reporting solution to understand:

- How sales and profitability are performing over time.
- Which sports and product categories contribute most to revenue.
- Which products generate higher gross profit.
- How performance differs across regions and stores.
- How sales growth compares with previous periods.
- How payment methods and product tiers contribute to business performance.

Without an interactive analytical solution, identifying performance trends, comparing business segments and investigating underperforming areas can require significant manual effort.

This project addresses these challenges through an interactive retail performance analytics solution built in Power BI.

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Analyze overall retail sales and profitability.
2. Monitor sales growth and year-over-year performance.
3. Evaluate gross profit and gross margin.
4. Identify high-performing sports, products and product tiers.
5. Compare sales and profitability across geographical regions.
6. Analyze payment method contributions.
7. Enable detailed regional analysis through drill-through functionality.
8. Provide interactive reporting with slicers, bookmarks and custom tooltips.
9. Deliver actionable business insights through management dashboards.

## 5. Key Business Questions

### Overall Business Performance
- What is the total net sales generated?
- How much gross profit does the business generate?
- What is the overall gross margin percentage?
- How many units have been sold?
- How many orders have been placed?
- How is sales performance changing year over year?

### Sales Performance
- How do sales change across months and quarters?
- Which payment methods contribute most to sales?
- Which sports generate the highest revenue?
- Which product types contribute significantly to sales?
- How does sales performance vary across product tiers?

### Product Performance
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which products contribute the most gross profit?
- How does product profitability vary?
- Which product tiers contribute most to revenue?

### Regional Performance
- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- Which regions contribute the most gross profit?
- How does product performance vary by region?
- Which products drive regional profitability?

## 6. Project Scope

### In Scope
- Data understanding and preparation.
- Data modelling and relationship development.
- Business KPI development.
- Sales and profitability analysis.
- Year-over-year performance analysis.
- Regional and product-level analysis.
- Interactive Power BI dashboard development.
- Drill-through and report-page tooltips.
- Business insights and recommendations.

### Out of Scope

- Inventory optimization.
- Customer-level segmentation.

These areas may be considered for future project enhancements.

## 7. Expected Business Outcomes

The completed analytics solution is expected to help stakeholders:

- Monitor sales and profitability from a centralized report.
- Identify important sales and profit contributors.
- Compare performance across regions and product segments.
- Investigate regional performance at a detailed level.
- Understand year-over-year business trends.
- Make more informed, data-driven business decisions.

## 8. Final Deliverable

An interactive Power BI report containing:

1. Executive Overview.
2. Sales Performance Analytics.
3. Regional Detailed Analysis with drill-through.
4. Custom Product Performance Report Tooltip.

The solution provides an integrated view of retail sales, profitability, product performance and regional business trends.
