# 06 – Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Document Purpose

This document presents the key business findings and recommendations from the Decathlon Retail Performance Analytics project. The analysis focuses on evaluating sales growth, profitability, product performance, and regional contribution to support business decision-making.

**Current reporting period:** 1 January 2026 – 15 September 2026  
**Comparison period:** 1 January 2025 – 15 September 2025

*September figures represent a partial month and are compared with the corresponding period in the previous year.*

## 2. Users and Decisions

| User | Decision supported |
|---|---|
| Retail leadership | Assess whether revenue and profit are improving against last year |
| Regional managers | Identify regional contribution and investigate state-level results |
| Merchandising team | Compare sports, brands, and product types for assortment review |
| Commercial analysts | Examine sales, margin, orders, and promotional segments together |

## 3. Business Questions

1. What are current YTD net sales and gross profit, and how do they compare with PYTD?
2. Is gross margin improving, and by how many percentage points?
3. How have units, order count, and average order value changed?
4. Which months show growth or decline against the same period last year?
5. Which sports generate the most YTD net sales?
6. Which product types combine strong sales with strong margins?
7. How much does each region contribute to YTD sales?
8. Which brands have the strongest and weakest YTD sales growth?
9. How do these answers change when a user applies the available filters?

## 4. Key Business Findings

| Performance Area | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Net Sales | ₹5.53 crore, increased by **24.33%** |
| Gross Profit | ₹2.17 crore, increased by **25.32%** |
| Gross Margin | Improved from 38.99% to **39.30%** (+0.31 percentage points) |
| Total Orders | Increased from 19,577 to 24,083 (**+23.02%**) |
| Units Sold | Increased from 38,759 to 47,767 (**+23.24%**) |
| Average Order Value | Increased from ₹2,270.31 to ₹2,294.56 (**+1.07%**) |
| Monthly Performance | All reported months recorded positive YoY sales growth. May had the strongest growth (+30.49%), while August had the weakest (+18.64%) |
| Sports Performance | Hiking generated the highest sales (₹1.21 crore), while Fitness recorded the highest gross margin (41.22%) |
| Product Type Performance | Camping Tent generated the highest sales (₹36.05 lakh), while Hiking Shoes generated the highest gross profit (₹14.66 lakh) |
| Regional Performance | South contributed 31.69% of sales, followed by West at 27.03%. Together, they contributed 58.72% |

## 5. Business Recommendations

| Priority | Recommendation | Business Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| High | Maintain the strong sales performance of Hiking and explore opportunities to grow Fitness sales | Hiking leads sports-level revenue, while Fitness has the highest gross margin |
| High | Review product costs, pricing, and discount strategies for lower-margin categories such as Cricket Ball, Rain Jacket, and Mountain Bike | These categories have comparatively lower gross margins and may offer opportunities for profitability improvement |
| High | Investigate August's lower gross margin alongside its sales and product mix | August recorded the highest monthly sales but the lowest monthly gross margin among the reported months. Review discounting and product mix before deciding on corrective action |
| Medium | Protect sales momentum in South and West while assessing opportunities in Central and East | South and West together contribute 58.72% of YTD sales |
| Medium | Investigate lower-sales stores using local demand, product mix, store format, and order performance | Store-level analysis can help identify whether differences are related to local demand or operational and assortment opportunities |
| Medium | Compare brand-level YoY sales growth before making brand assortment decisions | Brand-level growth figures were not included in the supplied SQL analysis results |

*Recommendations are proposed actions based on the observed results. Further investigation is required before attributing performance changes to a specific cause.*

The dashboard supports interactive exploration of the business questions defined in this document.

## 7. Conclusion

Decathlon recorded positive YTD performance, with net sales increasing by **24.33%** and gross profit increasing by **25.32%** compared with the previous year. Gross margin also improved by 0.31 percentage points.

The key business priorities are to sustain growth in strong-performing categories, improve profitability in lower-margin product types, investigate monthly margin changes, and identify regional and store-level opportunities.

The Power BI dashboard brings these findings together in an interactive format, helping business users monitor performance and make data-supported decisions.
